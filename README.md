# KontentHouse — Social Media Marketing & Management Agency

> Architectural content, founder-led branding, cinematic video production, performance Meta Ads, and revenue-focused social media management. Operating across Mumbai ↔ Dubai.

## 🚀 Live Demo & Preview
- Built with React 18, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Three.js, React Three Fiber, Lenis smooth scroll, and Framer Motion.

## 🌟 Core Agency Capabilities
- **Creative & Content Production:** 4K video cinematography, viral short-form editing, product photography, and high-retention scripting.
- **Revenue-Focused Strategy:** Uncovering operational sales peaks (e.g., late-night 4 AM dining) and deploying content calibrated for commercial return.
- **Founder-Led Branding & Stunts:** Positioning business founders as relatable industry authorities backed by viral marketing stunts.
- **Performance Marketing & Meta Ads:** High-conversion funnel architectures optimizing reported CPCs (e.g. ₹458 down to ₹7–₹10).
- **AI Integration & Digital Solutions:** Automated content research, AI workflows, and digital infrastructure.
- **White-Label Backend Production:** Elastic post-production engine for partner agencies scaling output without overhead.
